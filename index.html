Garment accessories.
Global expertise.

M Y & UNION HK LTD brings together garment accessory manufacturing expertise and an international network. From a distinctive button to the perfect buckle, we help apparel businesses find the finishing details their collections need.

Union International Accessories (PVT) Ltd and M Y & UNION HK LTD manufacture polyester and metal accessories, with production facilities in Bangladesh, China and Sri Lanka, and a head office in Hong Kong.
